Olivia Voldarenqueen of the hungry

Olivia Voldaren is the eccentric, immortal progenitor of the Voldaren vampire bloodline on Innistrad. Four cards show her under her own name, spanning 2011 to 2024: Olivia Voldaren, Olivia, Mobilized for War, Olivia, Crimson Bride, and Olivia, Opulent Outlaw.

Who she is

The MTG Wiki calls her “a vampire native from Innistrad and the progenitor of the Voldaren bloodline.” In life she was described as beautiful, strange, hermetic, and antisocial, preferring remote manor homes built for her with her seemingly boundless wealth. As a vampire she is a famous eccentric and bon vivant: the elite of vampire society know Olivia throws the best parties, and nobles happily travel to her seasonal balls.

Nature
Formerly human, now one of Innistrad’s oldest vampires — alive and active since before −2440 AR, a member of the Time of Legends.
Origin
Around −2421 AR she took part in Shilgengar’s Ritual, drinking the blood of the angel Marycz that Edgar Markov had drained, which turned her into an immortal vampire.
Bloodline
Progenitor of House Voldaren. Relatives include Florian and Relio Voldaren. Voldaren vampires tend to live apart from others, in distant manors at the borderlands of Innistrad’s provinces.
Method
She is able to fly, keeps a keen interest in watching the faithful lose their faith before death, and turns other creatures into vampires with her own blood.
Status
As of the Outlaws of Thunder Junction story (2024), she has left Innistrad for Thunder Junction, ruling a new crew of outlaws as she did her vampires at home.

Her story

Olivia’s major appearances run through the second half of the Innistrad story: an alliance of convenience with Sorin Markov that cost him his angel, a theft at the Harvesttide festival, an attempted takeover of Innistrad’s vampires through marriage to Edgar Markov, and a fresh start on Thunder Junction.

  1. A price for Sorin Olivia Sorin

    Resident planeswalker Sorin Markov seeks Olivia’s help in his quarrel with the lithomancer Nahiri. Olivia agrees — on the condition that Sorin kill his beloved angel Avacyn, whose madness is tearing Innistrad apart. Once Sorin fulfills the wish, the two assemble a vampire army and go to war against Nahiri.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  2. Self-proclaimed lord of Innistrad Olivia Sorin

    During the war, Olivia leads her vampire host against Nahiri’s cultists and intervenes when Nahiri confronts Sorin directly, telling her she can’t allow the revenge even though Sorin deserves it. Nahiri summons Eldrazi to occupy Olivia’s vampires while she duels Sorin alone, defeats him, and encases him in stone. Olivia proclaims herself the new lord of Innistrad and takes Sorin’s sword, the Parasite Blade, for herself.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  3. The Battle of Thraben Olivia

    Leaving Sorin for dead, Olivia and her army head to Thraben, where the plane’s defenders are making their last stand against the Eldrazi. She and the cathar Thalia agree a temporary truce to purge the city of monsters, and during the battle Olivia kills Seeta, leader of the Sinpurged, in front of Thalia.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  4. The Harvesttide theft Olivia

    Aware of Arlinn Kord’s plan to end the Eternal Night at the Harvesttide festival, Olivia chooses not to interfere — she’d rather let others do her work for her. As the ritual nears completion she sweeps down, holds Katilda hostage, and demands the Moonsilver Key. When Arlinn hands it over, Olivia hurls Katilda to the ground anyway, injuring both her and Arlinn, and leaves with the key.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  5. The wedding Olivia Edgar Sorin

    Olivia plans to marry Edgar Markov to bind House Markov to her and seize control of Innistrad’s vampire families, using Sigarda’s blood and the Moonsilver Key’s power to control the plane’s angels. She captures Sigarda, then uses her own blood to wake Edgar from his coffin-sleep, charming him into agreeing to marry her. During the ceremony Sorin attacks her; at the same moment Katilda’s spirit frees Sigarda from captivity, and the furious angel shatters the estate’s stained glass, hurting or killing many guests. Olivia and Edgar are not seriously hurt.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  6. The Gatewatch’s raid Olivia Edgar

    Sigarda’s outburst lowers the wards on Voldaren Manor, letting the Gatewatch raid the estate. Kaya breaks Olivia’s hold on her train of geists, forcing her to flee with Arlinn Kord in pursuit. Cornered, Olivia impales Arlinn’s hand after Arlinn loses control of her werewolf form, then escapes with the Moonsilver Key still in hand. Edgar later finds her and pleads with her to end their plans; discovered by Arlinn and the werewolf pack, Olivia drops the Key in defeat and flees with Edgar through a broken window.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

  7. A fresh start on Thunder Junction Olivia

    After discovering the Omenpaths, Olivia settles on Thunder Junction to clear her mind following the ruined wedding and failed takeover, while plotting her next move. She finds a population of frustrated outlaws who listen when she speaks, and once again becomes a queen of hungry, deadly subjects.

    MTG Wiki: Olivia Voldaren

This timeline rests on one primary source, the MTG Wiki’s Olivia Voldaren page, itself built from citations to the original Wizards of the Coast story chapters.

Playing her as a commander

Olivia, Opulent Outlaw is her most played build, leading 5,806 decks on EDHREC — built around treasure (657 decks), +1/+1 counters (233), the outlaws mechanic she shares with the rest of Thunder Junction (219), and go-wide tokens (215). Her original self, Olivia Voldaren, leads 2,911 decks, mostly a vampires tribal theme (240 decks) with a theft subtheme (81) built around her ability to steal other Vampires. Olivia, Crimson Bride leads a near-identical 2,910 decks, heavy in reanimator (270) and vampires (236). Olivia, Mobilized for War is the smallest build at 1,100 decks, favoring reanimator (108) and discard/madness (107 and 64). EDHREC counts decks of every pod size, not 1v1 specifically.

What could not be confirmed

  • Where her story goes next. The MTG Wiki leaves her settled on Thunder Junction as of 2024, ruling a new crew of outlaws; I found no later chapter.
  • The fate of the Moonsilver Key. She drops it during the Gatewatch’s raid and it’s later retrieved by Arlinn Kord and the Gatewatch, per the wiki, but I found no account of what became of it after that.
  • Associated-but-not-depicting cards. The wiki lists several cards named for her (Olivia’s Attendants, Olivia’s Bloodsworn, Olivia’s Dragoon, Bride’s Gown, Voldaren Estate) as “associated” rather than “represented in” — I treated that distinction as meaning they don’t depict her directly, so they’re not given full entries above.
