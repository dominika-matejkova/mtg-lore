Dihadabinder of wills

Geyadrone Dihada is an ancient demonic planeswalker who rules through corrupted champions. Two planeswalker cards show her: Geyadrone Dihada (Modern Horizons 2, 2021) and Dihada, Binder of Wills (Dominaria United Commander, 2022). They are the same character.

Who she is

The MTG Wiki calls her an ancient demonic shapeshifter planeswalker who first appeared on Dominaria between the fall of the Thran and the Brothers’ War. She collects powerful beings and corrupts or binds them to her will. The CoolStuffInc lore article says she was created for the Armada comics.

Nature
A demonic planeswalker. Magic Untapped describes her as having the upper body of a woman and legs that end in octopus-like tentacles.
Method
She rules through proxies. A Wizards of the Coast feature on Dominaria United legends says: “In the past, she has sought to control the worlds she visits by corrupted proxies such as Sol’Kanar or Dakkon Blackblade.”
Her proxies
Dakkon Blackblade, an armorer she made into her champion, and Sol’Kanar, a protector of the Corondor forest Khone whom the Blackblade turned into a swamp king.
Appears in
The Armada comic Dakkon Blackblade #1 and the Battlemage video game, per the CoolStuffInc article.
The name
None of the pages I read explains what “Geyadrone” means.
Status
Alive and active in Dominaria United, with her goals unclear (see her story below).

Her story

Her best-documented story is the Blackblade–Dihada War on Corondor, which ended with Dakkon bound to her. She then left Dominaria, resurfaced in the Planeswalkers’ War, and turned up again at the Tolarian Academy during the Phyrexian incursion of Dominaria United.

  1. The commission Dihada

    Dihada visits the armorer Dakkon and commissions the most powerful sword ever made, paying with planeswalker power. She teaches him the enchantment for a blade that steals the soul of whoever it kills. He spends years sacrificing people to it, and the sources say he cooled it with a slave each time it was heated, including, in one telling, his own son. When he hands it over, she uses it on him. His planeswalker spark ignites as the blade takes his soul, and he is bound to the sword.

    Magic Untapped · CoolStuffInc

  2. Sol’Kanar and Corondor Dihada

    Dihada takes the Blackblade to Khone, a forest on the island of Corondor, and gives it to its protector. The blade corrupts him into Sol’Kanar. She turns the forest into a salt marsh and sends him against the island’s civilizations. He builds the Unholy Citadel from the remains of his victims.

    Magic Untapped · MTG Wiki

  3. Carth summons Dakkon Dakkon

    A young man named Carth tries to assassinate Dihada and is imprisoned. A mad monk tells him Dakkon’s story and helps him escape. Carth then summons Dakkon, who is bound to his service. The two attack Dihada’s citadel, retreat to Khone, and are ambushed there. Dakkon disarms Sol’Kanar and Carth takes up the Blackblade.

    CoolStuffInc: the war

  4. The elder dragons Dihada Dakkon

    Dihada summons two elder dragons against Dakkon. He kills Piru, and the blast opens a huge chasm. Dihada absorbs the dragon’s power and reveals that she was the mad monk all along. She captures and binds Dakkon, promising to summon him again. Carth leaves, and Dakkon calls him “Carth the Lion”.

    CoolStuffInc · MTG Wiki · MTGGoldfish

  5. The Planeswalkers’ War Dihada

    Dihada leaves Dominaria and comes back during the Planeswalkers’ War. Magic Untapped says the mad planeswalker Ravidel summoned her to Corondor with a device called the Mox Beacon, together with Leshrac, Tevesh Szat, Kristina of the Woods, Ash Warlord Embereck, Sandruu and Jared Carthalion, and that she survived. The CoolStuffInc article says the war comes from the Battlemage game, where she goes after Carth’s descendant Jared Carthalion to reclaim Dakkon as her “chief lieutenant”. The game’s lore says “the cleverness of Carth and the power of Dakkon’s black blade proved to be Dihada’s undoing”, yet the same article says her actual fate is unknown.

    Magic Untapped · CoolStuffInc

  6. Dominaria United Dihada

    She reappears in the Phyrexian incursion at the Tolarian Academy at Lat-Nam. Two sources say she killed or removed the archmage in charge and took his place leading the school. The MTG Wiki adds that she took over Corondor, hoping to force a conflict with Jared Carthalion that would bring Dakkon back under her control. Whether she works with the Phyrexians or simply seized an opening is not settled.

    Magic Untapped · MTG Wiki

Where the sources disagree

  • Which elder dragons. The CoolStuffInc article names Chromium and Piru. The MTG Wiki says Piru and Rhuell, and MTGGoldfish says Piru and “Chromium Rhuell”. The MTG Wiki itself does not say whether Chromium and Rhuell are one dragon or two. Only Piru’s death is consistent across all three.
  • What the sword did to Dakkon. Magic Untapped says she ran him through, igniting his spark as the blade took his soul. CoolStuffInc and MTGGoldfish say she struck or used the blade on his shadow, stealing his soul that way.
  • Her motives toward Phyrexia. The Wizards feature says she left Dominaria at the end of the Planeswalker War, a few years before the first Phyrexian invasion, and that some believe she still lives, diminished, and secretly influences Dominarian politics. The MTG Wiki has her back for the later incursion.

I read these pages through a page-summary tool, so check quotes against the originals before relying on exact wording. The CoolStuffInc article calls the comic synopsis non-canonical.

Cards that depict her

Dominaria United Commander · 2022

Dihada, Binder of Wills

{1}{R}{W}{B}Legendary Planeswalker — Dihada · loyalty 5 · mythic

+2 Up to one target legendary creature gains vigilance, lifelink, and indestructible until your next turn.

−3 Reveal the top four cards of your library. Put any number of legendary cards from among them into your hand and the rest into your graveyard. Create a Treasure token for each card put into your graveyard this way.

−11 Gain control of all nonland permanents until end of turn. Untap them. They gain haste until end of turn.

Dihada, Binder of Wills can be your commander.

Art: Néstor Ossandón Leal. Official rulings: none.

Modern Horizons 2 · 2021

Geyadrone Dihada

{1}{U}{B}{R}Legendary Planeswalker — Dihada · loyalty 4 · mythic

Protection from permanents with corruption counters on them

+1 Each opponent loses 2 life and you gain 2 life. Put a corruption counter on up to one other target creature or planeswalker.

−3 Gain control of target creature or planeswalker until end of turn. Untap it and put a corruption counter on it. It gains haste until end of turn.

−7 Gain control of each permanent with a corruption counter on it.

Art: Ekaterina Burmak. Legal in Commander, Legacy, Vintage and Modern.

Three official rulings (18 June 2021): she can’t be targeted, dealt damage or enchanted by permanents with corruption counters, and creatures with them can still attack her but their damage is prevented. Permanents keep their counters if she leaves, so a later Geyadrone Dihada will have protection from them too.

Dihada, Binder of Wills is the Commander-focused one: Mardu (red, white, black) and able to lead a deck. Geyadrone Dihada is Grixis (blue, black, red) and is a regular Modern Horizons 2 mythic.

Playing her as a commander

Dihada, Binder of Wills is legal in Commander and leads 11,157 decks on EDHREC. The first theme listed is Legends (1,424 decks), followed by reanimator (569), historic (331), combo (242) and treasure (190). EDHREC counts all Commander decks, so these numbers are not specific to 1v1.

Her loyalty starts at 5, so three +2 activations take her to 11 for the −11. That is my arithmetic from the card text, not something EDHREC or Wizards says.
