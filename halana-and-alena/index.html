Halana & Alenarangers of the Ulvenwald

Halana and Alena are a pair of human rangers from Kessig province on Innistrad — partners in both work and life who hunt werewolves and protect travelers through the Ulvenwald forest. Three cards depict them: one for each of them solo, and one merged card showing them fighting as a single unit.

Who they are

The MTG Wiki describes Halana (“Hal”) and Alena as trackers, hunters, and protectors who live in the depths of the dark Ulvenwald forest on Innistrad, guiding travelers safely to the nearby town of Gatstaf. The wiki explicitly calls them partners “in both work and life,” and lists them under its LGBT characters category — they’re a couple.

Origin
Kessig province, Innistrad — the same wooded region as Arlinn Kord and much of the werewolf storyline.
Halana’s role
An archer and ranger, skilled with reach and precision strikes from a distance.
Alena’s role
A trapper and scout, described as highly skilled at tracking by senses alone — particularly good at hunting werewolves.
Specialty
Defending travelers through the Ulvenwald from werewolves, vampires, and the possessed.
Status
Active as of Innistrad: Crimson Vow (2021), the most recent story appearance found for either of them.

Their story

Compared to the other characters in this archive, Halana and Alena are minor, flavor-level figures — the MTG Wiki documents only two story appearances for them, both supporting-cast roles in larger Innistrad crises rather than their own dedicated arc. I'm stating that plainly rather than padding it out: what follows is everything the wiki records.

  1. Hunting Emrakul’s cultists Halana Alena

    When Emrakul’s presence on Innistrad spreads cult worship and bizarre bodily corruption near the Ulvenwald, Halana and Alena track the cultists' activity and confront them directly. Facing a threat bigger than their usual quarry, the two rangers set aside their long feud with the local werewolves and join forces with the lycanthropes to fight the corrupted cultists and, eventually, Emrakul's influence itself.

    MTG Wiki: Halana · MTG Wiki: Alena (citing “Under the Silver Moon,” 2016, and “Emrakul Rises,” 2016)

  2. Back to war with the werewolves Halana Alena

    By the time of Innistrad: Crimson Vow, with Emrakul's threat resolved and the plane plunged into the Eternal Night, the truce between the rangers and the werewolves has broken down. The wiki notes only that their battles against the lycanthropes “have resumed” — no further detail on specific events is given.

    MTG Wiki: Halana · MTG Wiki: Alena (citing “The Legends of Innistrad: Crimson Vow,” 2021)

Both MTG Wiki pages (Halana, Alena) tell this same brief history nearly word for word, built from citations to Wizards of the Coast's own story articles; I read the wiki pages rather than the original articles themselves.

Playing them as commanders

All three cards here can lead a Commander deck — but by two different rules, which is worth untangling. Halana, Kessig Ranger and Alena, Kessig Trapper are each legendary creatures with the keyword ability Partner: that lets a player use both of them together as two separate commanders in one deck, instead of the usual single commander. Halana and Alena, Partners is a plain legendary creature with no Partner text at all — but it doesn’t need any. Any legendary creature can be your sole commander by default; special “can be your commander” text (as seen on some planeswalkers elsewhere in this archive) is only needed for card types that aren’t normally commander-eligible, like planeswalkers. A legendary creature needs nothing extra.

On EDHREC, the merged Foundations card leads 4,549 decks, almost all built around +1/+1 counters (653 of those decks tag that theme specifically) and aggressive, hasty creature strategies. The Halana/Alena Partner pairing is tracked separately and far more niche, appearing as a commander pairing in only 16 decks on EDHREC. EDHREC's numbers cover decks of every pod size, not 1v1 specifically.
