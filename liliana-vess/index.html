Liliana Vessthe Necromancer

Liliana Vess is a human former planeswalker, master necromancer, and one of the five original planeswalker cards ever printed. Eight cards show her across nearly two decades of story, from a desperate attempt to save her brother’s life through a demonic pact that cost her decades of servitude, to a late-life redemption as a professor who finally stopped running from her past.

Who she is

The MTG Wiki describes Liliana as a tall woman with black hair, violet eyes, and purple tattoos that trace a demonic contract across her skin — lines that glow when she casts powerful spells and bleed when she draws on the Chain Veil. She commands black mana to reanimate the dead, corrupt the living, and unlock power from death itself, and though she looks to be in her late twenties, she is actually over two centuries old.

Age
Born before 4357 AR in Benalia, Caligo Forest, Dominaria — roughly 205 years old as of her most recent appearances, having lost her planeswalker spark in the Great Desparking during the Phyrexian invasion of Arcavios.
Family
Her brother Josu Vess was fatally cursed by Liliana’s own botched cure and later raised as a lich by the Cabal; she was forced to destroy him herself on Dominaria.
Temperament
Charismatic, witty, and profoundly cynical — she viewed most people as stepping stones to power for most of her life, though her time with the Gatewatch and her guilt over Gideon Jura’s death pushed her toward genuine (if reluctant) loyalty.
Signature relic
The Chain Veil, an artifact of the ancient Onakke civilization that amplifies her necromancy at the cost of her body and sanity; she carried it for years before finally giving it up on Fiora.
Status
Alive, de-sparked, and teaching necromancy as Professor Liliana Vess at Strixhaven University on Arcavios.

Her story

Liliana’s is one of the longest-running character arcs in Magic — a healer turned necromancer, bound by a demonic pact she spent decades trying to escape, who ultimately betrayed a god-dragon to save the friends she always claimed not to need. This timeline covers the major beats; side material like the alternate-reality “Echoverse” version of her and the ambiguous, hard-to-reconcile Test of Metal novel appearance are omitted for length and flagged below.

  1. A curse and a spark Liliana

    Raised under her general father’s thumb in Benalia and trained as a healer in the Forward Order, Liliana secretly studies necromancy, believing it will sharpen her healing craft. When her brother Josu is struck by a magical illness, a mysterious stranger (later revealed as the Raven Man) tricks her into using a corrupted cure. It saves Josu’s body but drives him insane; forced to kill the monster he becomes, Liliana’s grief and horror ignite her planeswalker spark.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  2. Crushed by Sorin Markov Liliana Sorin

    Landing on Innistrad, Liliana studies under vampires and liches to perfect her necromancy, refusing to join them in true death. She encounters the plane’s vampire lord, Sorin Markov, who defeats her easily, calls her too weak to be a threat, and warns her to be a civil guest or die.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  3. Bound to four demons Liliana Bolas

    Weakened after the Mending, Liliana is approached by a vastly more powerful Nicol Bolas, who brokers a pact between her and four demons — Kothophed, Griselbrand, Razaketh, and Belzenlok — restoring her youth and a fraction of her power in exchange for servitude, the debts etched into her skin as glowing tattoos.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  4. Betraying Jace on Ravnica Liliana Jace

    On Ravnica, Liliana seduces the mind mage Jace Beleren, staying at his side through an identity crisis — but her loyalty is secretly directed by Bolas, and she maneuvers Jace into a fight against Tezzeret that collapses the Infinite Consortium. She considers running away with Jace more than once, but her fear of her demons always wins out.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  5. Claiming the Chain Veil Liliana

    Sent by Kothophed to retrieve the Onakke Chain Veil on Shandalar, Liliana kills Garruk Wildspeaker’s quarry along the way, unknowingly earning the planeswalker’s lasting hatred. Once she holds the Veil, she turns its power on Kothophed himself rather than surrender it, but the artifact’s whispering spirits begin to make her its vessel as much as its wielder.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  6. The Sundering of the Helvault Liliana Thalia

    Hunting her demon master Griselbrand on Innistrad, Liliana arrives at the Siege of Thraben, resurrects a dead church official to learn Griselbrand’s prison is the Helvault, then forces the choice onto the Cathar Thalia — destroy the vault or lose her soldiers. Thalia saves her men; the Helvault shatters, freeing both Griselbrand and the archangel Avacyn. Liliana uses the Veil to kill Griselbrand for good.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  7. Joining the Gatewatch Liliana Gatewatch

    When Emrakul arrives on Innistrad, Liliana raises a zombie army against the Eldrazi Horrors, drawing more deeply on the Chain Veil than ever before and nearly dying in the process. Seeing the value of allies against her own enemies, she joins the Gatewatch after Emrakul is sealed away — intending to direct them toward her own ends.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  8. Devoured on Amonkhet Liliana Jace

    Swallowed whole by a wurm on arrival at Amonkhet, Liliana decomposes it from the inside with the Chain Veil. She later tears apart her demon master Razaketh with an army of risen Luxa creatures, settling one more of her four debts — but the Gatewatch is routed by Bolas soon after, and she flees, knowing she now owes the dragon her life.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  9. Destroying her own brother Liliana Gideon

    Returning to her ruined homeland of Vess on Dominaria, Liliana discovers that her brother Josu has been raised as a lich by the Cabal’s cultists and used as bait to keep her from fighting their master, Belzenlok. With only Gideon Jura standing by her, she destroys the lich herself; dying, Josu curses her for bringing their house to ruin. She and Gideon then help kill Belzenlok before Nicol Bolas reveals the demons’ pact has defaulted to him.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  10. Dreadhorde general, then defector Liliana Gideon

    Installed by Bolas as general of his undead Dreadhorde on Ravnica, Liliana quietly minimizes the carnage she’s forced to inflict, then turns all of Bolas’s Eternals against him outright, breaking her contract. The broken pact begins aging her to death on the spot — until Gideon transfers the curse onto himself, sacrificing his life so she can survive and help finish off the Elder Dragon.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  11. Hunted, and giving up the Veil Liliana Kaya

    Blamed by Ravnica’s guilds for the war’s casualties, Liliana flees and is hunted by the planeswalker Kaya. Cornered by an imposter wearing her own name and face on Dominaria, she agrees to let Kaya kill her in exchange for freeing her people — survives through a twist involving a bound djinn — and finally, after much hesitation, drops the Chain Veil for good. She adopts the alias Ana Iora and disappears from the Multiverse’s view.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

  12. Professor Onyx, and a lost spark Liliana

    Settling on Arcavios as “Professor Serafina Onyx,” Liliana teaches necromancy at Strixhaven, fights off an Oriq uprising and later the Phyrexian invasion of the plane, and loses her ability to planeswalk entirely in the Great Desparking. She eventually drops the alias, reclaims her true name, and keeps teaching — trying, for the first time, to be someone worth trusting.

    MTG Wiki: Liliana Vess

Playing her as a commander

Only Liliana, Heretical Healer can lead a Commander deck on her own. None of her seven solo planeswalker cards carry “can be your commander” text, so they’re Commander-legal to play inside a deck but can’t serve as the commander itself outside the Oathbreaker format — Heretical Healer starts as a legendary creature and transforms into a planeswalker, which is what makes her eligible, the same situation found on Sorin Markov’s page in this archive. She leads 5,579 decks on EDHREC, built around reanimator strategies (331 decks), zombies (319), discard (241), and planeswalkers as a subtheme (215). Liliana, Dreadhorde General is by far the most-played Liliana planeswalker inside those decks, appearing in 76.4% of them. EDHREC counts decks of every pod size, not 1v1 specifically.
